# 🎬 Netflix Dataset - Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Exploratory Data Analysis performed on the Netflix Movies and TV Shows dataset to uncover content trends, rating distributions, country-wise contributions, and release patterns.

---

## 📌 Objective

To explore and analyze the Netflix dataset and extract meaningful insights about:

* Netflix content distribution
* Movies vs TV Shows analysis
* Country-wise content contribution
* Content rating distribution
* Release year trends
* Netflix catalog growth patterns
* Genre distribution

---

## 📂 Dataset

* netflix_titles.csv

Source: Kaggle Netflix Movies and TV Shows Dataset

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Movies dominate the Netflix catalog compared to TV Shows.
* The United States contributes the highest number of Netflix titles.
* India is the second-largest contributor of Netflix content.
* TV-MA is the most common rating category across Netflix.
* TV Shows are generally newer than Movies.
* South Korea and Japan show a relatively higher proportion of TV Shows.
* Most Netflix content was released after 2010.
